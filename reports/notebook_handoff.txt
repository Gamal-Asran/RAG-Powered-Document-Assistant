KNOWN LIMITATIONS
- q04 and q08 used the generation output budget without a final answer; their errors, thinking, and null scores are preserved. Do not retry them automatically.
- q08 is a document-level retrieval miss: AI RMF and Playbook chunks outranked the expected GenAI Profile. Similarities around 0.52–0.55 do not guarantee the right document or passage.
- q11 and q12 appended explanatory content to the abstention sentence, failing exact abstention. These failures remain even where the model judge was permissive.
- Retrieval pollution: 6 divider/near-empty results and 2 likely bibliography results among 48. Flags are explicit heuristics, not a filtering rule. No chunks were deleted.
- Strict citation formatting fails for some answers that add a literal chunk_id label. Historical citation scores are preserved; the smoke test checks source membership, not overall answer quality.
- Answerable questions q06 and q07 abstained. Saved prompt excerpts were shortened in 24/48 blocks to meet the context/output budget; full retrieved text is retained for review.
- Fourteen of 48 retrieved chunks exceed the original wrapper's 254-content-token allowance. The override to 452 avoids truncating 450-token embeddings within the underlying 512 limit; no shorter-window ablation established a quality gain or loss.
- Generator and judge use the same model. Some judge coverage/reasoning was inconsistent. Deterministic guards handle exact abstention and invalid citations after successful JSON validation; original judge output is retained. These scores are not independent human validation.
- This is a prototype, not a production-optimized or production-ready service. Known failures do not block building the backend/frontend prototype. Do not tune prompts, reindex, or rerun full evaluation merely to improve the saved scores.
